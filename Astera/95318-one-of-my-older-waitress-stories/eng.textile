Author: Astera

Been a while, this is one of my stories back from my time as a waitress.

Context: this is a dennys and anyone who’s anyone knows dennys isn’t exactly a good place in the modern era.

It was a morning breakfast shift which is always thr worst at a breakfast place. Me and my coworker who we will call Anne(not her real name) were the only 2 working the floor at the time since someone got really sick. Anyway we were unusually busy, a lot of times it was a graveyard in there. I had peed just before the rush which in hindsight was big brain because we wouldn’t get another chance for about 3 hours.

40 or so minutes in Anne and I are doing our thing, we do a few tables and i notice her being a little bouncy. My brain at the time was dead so I didnt think much of it. About 30 minutes later another coworker finally showed up and i noticed Anne get ready to use the restroom when a family came in, me and the other guy were already busy so she took them. She passed me in the kitchen area and i could tell she was desperate with her jittery movements. Shift continued in absolute hectic chaos until about 11am when there was finally a break. 

Anne who is visably desperate bolts to a bathroom after giving a table their orders, im doing my own thing and as i go back towards a table to do the “how are you doing” that absolutely no one likes i saw her holding herself in the corridor, i thought absolutely nothing of it. A little bit later Anne pulls me aside in the kitchen asking me or “john”(alias for the other guy) to take her tables. Me being dumb morning brain asked why and her face lit up and she quietly muttered “i totally wet myself”. Omo brain fired right there but i was too tired and just said i’d take care of it. She emerged from the bathroom 10 or so minutes later.

Work calmed down to almost a calm lul until my shift ended, we were all pretty close so i went to say bye to those i saw, when i got to anne she still looked visibly uncomfortable. Im not sure if she ever changed or not, pants were dark black so you probably couldnt tell eitherways.

To my knowledge she only told me and the head chef who she was close too. She quit a month later

Maybe a boring story and i may have forgotten some things, my memory is shotty sometimes
