I got permission from Selena to post her story from my perspective with some incriminating evidence. She’s still a little shy so she decided to let me go ahead and post for her.

So, my partner Selena had to work a 9-5 shift the other day, and we agreed to try and have a little fun, or, as we like to call it, play a little.

The rules were very simple since she was at work. We decided that we would give her the morning to get into the swing of things and have a final “reset” as she calls it to get herself in the right mindset. After her lunch break, she wouldn’t be allowed to use the bathroom anymore.

Now for most people this would sound like a simple challenge, but Selena’s bladder is probably around the size of a peanut. A large peanut, perhaps, but still a peanut. If you’ve read our last IRL experience, you would know that she struggles.

So, with the challenge set, I was excited, and so was Selena. What could possibly go wrong? Everything would go okay, right? Right.

Selena started off her morning as she usually does – with a festively made holiday coffee. Her current weapon, or drink of choice, is a gingerbread latte. Of course she refuses to have a normal sized coffee and consistently opts for 32 ounces of liquid to get through her morning. It’s excessive, but she allows herself to have such a large drink in moderation. Considering it was a special occasion, I decided to let it slide. I would call it a bad idea on a normal day, but what do I know? After downing her drink, she hopefully just stuck to water.

She confessed that the building’s heat was on the fritz and that it was considerably warmer than normal. The weather has already been fickle lately and the outside temperature was already considerably warm. Between Selena’s old building and broken heater and the weather, she was boiling hot. She had to have guzzled at least six bottles of water before even going on her break though she confessed she wasn’t too sure how much she had. Selena confessed that she could hardly keep track because of the heat and since she uses a reusable water bottle and takes advantage of the water bottle filling station, she couldn’t remember. At the very least I knew the challenge would be well on track by the time she returned back to the building. Selena was looking at an all-afternoon hold that would challenge her for a bit.

Unfortunately for me, she didn’t exactly keep up to date on things when she returned to work after lunch. Things started to get busy, but she told me that due to a person wearing an extra double heavy dose of aftershave or cologne, her allergies kicked into gear.

This definitely made things more interesting for me and far more complicated for Selena and her holding ability. Sneezes have always tended to be dangerous for her and have more than once led to her saying to me, “I just peed myself a little.”

Finally, the clock hit 5 and she could punch out of work to pick up dinner and head home. Selena told me she was already super desperate, a solid 8.5/10 at bare minimum and she still had to get dinner.

About thirty minutes later, Selena finally pulled back up to the house. At this point, it was clear that she was super desperate. She could barely keep still and kept bouncing around, but Selena couldn’t yet get to the bathroom. Since we were currently in the company of her mother, we needed to eat dinner first. Selena tried to eat as quickly as she could, which still wasn’t very fast, but she tried.

To Selena’s credit, she did a fair job of hiding how badly she needed to pee once she sat down, and it was even better hidden because she could blame her struggling on her allergies. It was obvious that they had hit hard, but she must have been at least a 9.5, and she needed to go very badly by that point.

After dinner, she rushed quickly as she could upstairs to grab a change of clothes and raced back downstairs to the bathroom.

From what Selena told me, she got her pants off before realizing that there was absolutely no way she was making those last few steps to the toilet. She decided to bail out and jumped into the shower to try and keep the rug outside of the shower from getting soaked. And well, the result of that is more than obvious. I don’t think that I’ve ever seen her more relaxed than after she got out of the shower.

I hope that everyone enjoyed our little moment of playtime together. We certainly did.
