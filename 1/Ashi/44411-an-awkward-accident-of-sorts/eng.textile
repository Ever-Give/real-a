Before I even explain what happened to me today, I want to reiterate...

IN NO WAY IS IT ACCEPTABLE TO INTENTIONALLY USE THE WRONG BATHROOM - NO.

What I'm about to explain was completely accidental.

So, today was my second day at the new workplace. I got a tour yesterday and was shown around, where the bathrooms are (by the canteen) and all the codes to get into the staff areas and such.

I've just gotten back into store after heading out for lunch, feeling pretty desperate to pee, I decide it's probably best to relieve myself before I go back on shift.

I head down the corridor I'm familiar with, and find myself at the canteen/break room. Looking further down the corridor I catch sight of the generic 'image' of the toilet sign, y'know, standing figure shape and rush in. As I go in, I notice there's only 2 cubicles, one currently in use. I dismiss it as just being a small bathroom and rush in, lock the door and have the most blissful piss from having held in an iced tea and two drinks worth of water for over half of my shift... I hear the person in the cubicle next to me leave and I'm snapped out of my bubble of contentment and figure that I should do the same and hurry up... I finish up my business and leave the cubicle.

So I'm washing my hands and as I start looking around for the paper towels to dry my hands I notice there are three urinals on the wall. I literally did a double take as I suddenly realised I was in the mens bathroom and not the womens. I thought "oh shit" and immediately rushed for the exit, only to hear male voices right on the other side of the door.

The bathroom was laid out quite unusual - It had a few lockers on the opposite wall of the urinals, and there was a little corner you had to turn to get to the door. So I took a few steps back (incase the door opened) and I waited until the noise had passed and it sounded like the corridor was clear. As I stepped out there was a woman right outside the door, writing on a whiteboard next to the break room door.

I double checked the sign to confirm my suspicions, and bolted down the corridor and onto the shop floor. The woman probably saw me, but there are so many members of staff, and I work in a different department to 80% of the staff there, she probably didn't care.
