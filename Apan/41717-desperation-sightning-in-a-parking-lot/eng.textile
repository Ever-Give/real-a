Desperation sightning in a parking lot

Author: Apan

*000*2018-01-06 21:51

Right, there's not much at all to this one but I figured I'd post it anyway. A couple of months ago I had a gig with my band at a community center in the city and sometime around noon after setting up our gear, I went off to park my car in a nearby parking lot outside a second hand shop. While waiting for the parking ticket machine in a queue with maybe 5-6 people, I stood behind a woman about 45-50 years of age, but very good looking for her age. Blonde shoulder-length hair and a knee-length flowery skirt with black tights underneath, and as it was quite cold outside, a thick jacket which effectively concealed whatever she was wearing on top (bummer).

What instantly caught my attention was that she couldn't stand still for a second. One could put it down to the cold weather but her overall movements did not thoroughly fit in with that, not least since her upper body didn't display any shivering from being cold. Her legs on the other hand went back and forth, her feet stamping on the ground and her knees rubbing against each other. As the queue advanced she occasionally scissored her legs around each other as she inched forward before resuming her fidgeting. On a couple of occasions, she turned her head sideways as if looking for something and I probed her facial expression for something disclosing her desperation and there might have been a hint of worry in her face as her gaze appeared quite alarmed. I also heard some impatient sighs regularly escape her during the queuing time.

The queue lasted for maybe five minutes before she reached the ticket machine and as she paid and waited for her ticket, she continued lifting her legs alternately while nervously stomping her feet on the ground. After fetching her ticket and walking to her car it was my turn to fetch mine. I kept half an eye on her, not wanting to make it too obvious, and noticed that she went into the second hand shop at which point I had to leave to rejoin the band in the nearby bulding. That's where the story ended but the potential twist is that if she went in to the second hand shop in order to use their toilet, she would have been mightily disappointed since there are no bathrooms in there.

Knowing this sparked my imagination of what situation could have ensued. The only nearby public bathroom I can think of is the train station across the main road on the other side of said shop, but it is a bit of a hassle getting there and on the parking side of the shop is the inner city and during that time of day the only open places that might have public bathrooms are the restaurants, the nearest of which were maybe a couple of blocks away. I pondered upon elaborating the story from these circumstances and make it a semi-fiction for that forum section, but decided to deliver it unadorned anyway, leaving any additional imagination to the reader.
