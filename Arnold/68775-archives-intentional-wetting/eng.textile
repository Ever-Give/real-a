A while back there was a post asking about toilet accidents in school and whether people get punished for them. The writer expressed surprised that a girl in the choir with her daughter got detention or something for going in her pants at the school’s holiday concert. Well, I can honestly say that I’ve never heard of anyone being punished at school for having an ACCIDENT. But I can tell you a story of a girl at school who did get indeed get punished for wetting her pants.

When I was in high school, there were lots of issues with students drinking. Much of it was exaggerated, but drinking was indeed something that a lot of kids in my high school did and that included kids on sports teams. I played basketball and my senior year, they made all athletes sign a consent form agreeing that they could be tested for drugs and alcohol at any time. Well, one afternoon several weeks into the basketball season, they sprung a surprised test on us. Under the guise of a team meeting, they had us meeting in the girls’ locker room and they took us in groups of 3 over to the toilets to produce a urine sample. Personally, I thought it pretty stupid to be doing it after school like this because who’d have even gotten the chance to drink being in school all day.

However, one girl on the team had been drinking. Apparently she had snuck out at lunchtime and had several drinks, Apparently, there was still alcohol in her system and consequently she was desperate not to give a urine sample. Well, I suppose desperate times call for desperate measures. Suddenly, this girl just completely wets her pants -- probably figuring that they couldn’t do a drug test on her if she’d urinated in her pants and therefore they’d have nothing left to test. She tried to make it look like she’d just had an accident but the whole thing was just so obviously staged that we all knew better. And, of course, they just made her drink more and more water until she was eventually able to produce another urine sample. She tested positive for alcohol which resulted in her removal from the team and a five-day out of school suspension. And they also gave her two days of in-school suspension for purposely wetting her pants -- “Inappropriate Urination” they called it.
